How Every Price Actually Works

A $29 plan giving you 600 credits and a $29 plan giving you 10 minutes are not the same deal. The sticker price tells you almost nothing because five of the six main platforms meter your usage in credits, and a credit is almost never one video.

The formula that matters: price per finished minute of video. This report computes it from official tier data live-fetched on 21 August 2026.

The Math Behind the Numbers

HeyGen: 600 monthly credits ÷ 20 credits/min (Avatar IV) = 30 finished minutes. $29 ÷ 30 = $0.97/minute. On annual billing: $24 ÷ 30 = $0.80/minute. Standard avatars: 600 ÷ 3 = 200 minutes = $0.15/minute.

D-ID: $16 ÷ 15 minutes = $1.07/minute. Lite tier at $4.70 gives 10 minutes but stays watermarked and personal-use only — disqualified for social posting.

Vidnoz: 15 credits/month ≈ 15 minutes on Starter. $19.99 ÷ 15 = $1.33/minute. Extra credits cost $2 each. Voice cloning is a $9.99/month add-on not included in Starter.

Synthesia: Starter $29 ÷ 10 minutes = $2.90/minute. On annual billing approximately $18/month ÷ 10 = $1.80/minute. Each b-roll action costs 96 credits beyond the base.

The Rest of the Field

Creatify ($39/mo, 100 credits): UGC-ad factory. 5 credits per 15 seconds, so a 30-second ad is 10 credits ($3.90). At high-quality settings, Starter yields about 5 videos a month — roughly $7.80 per finished minute. Best for product ads.

Arcads ($110/mo, 8,000 credits): Most realistic talking-actor footage at agency pricing. About $11 per clip. Trap: multi-actor minutes multiply. No free trial.

Zeely ($79.95/mo, 10,000 credits): Takes 12% of your Meta ad spend on top. Cost rises with your budget. Avoid.

Captions ($24.99/mo Max): Editing and captioning tool first. Not a generator.

Argil ($39/mo, 1,600 credits): Pay-as-you-go credits never expire. Less established; quality unverified.

Fine-Print Traps

HeyGen

Charges for failed renders — about 30% of negative Trustpilot reviews cite this.

15-day refund window voided by any content creation.

"Unlimited" quietly became credit-metered mid-subscription in November 2025 (BBB complaints).

Vidnoz

Free accounts wiped after 2 weeks of inactivity.

Deleting projects does not restore credits.

Expressive avatars burn 4× credits (2/sec vs 0.5/sec).

Voice clone ($9.99/mo) and avatar ($299/yr) are add-ons.

D-ID

Credits expire after 12 months even when rolled over.

Lite tier keeps the watermark and restricts to personal use.

Editor is intentionally thin — no templates, no stock assets.

Synthesia

Minutes never roll over.

Custom Studio Avatar is $1,000, annual plans only.

Each b-roll action costs 96 credits beyond the base.

Method and Limitations

Prices verified on official pricing pages live 21 August 2026 (Tier A for all four main services). Quality claims are directional — most third-party comparisons (Tier C) rank HeyGen highest for lip-sync realism, but no independent lab has validated exact scores under controlled conditions.

Vidnoz Trustpilot 2.3/5 reflects documented billing and cancellation complaints (Tier B). D-ID credits expire after 12 months; Lite tier is watermarked and personal-use only (Tier A). No current open-source avatar model runs without an NVIDIA GPU.
