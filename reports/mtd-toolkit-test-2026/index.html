Research Toolkit Test

This report is a toolkit smoke test: it proves the three research libraries (trafilatura, pdfplumber, plotly) work end-to-end inside the deep-research pipeline — extract, analyze, render, deploy. The topic is real (UK MTD for Income Tax) and every figure below came through the libraries being tested.

Key findings

  1. Thresholds and start dates (extracted with trafilatura from GOV.UK): qualifying income over £50,000 → MTD from 6 April 2026; over £30,000 → 6 April 2027; over £20,000 → 6 April 2028. All three thresholds and dates appeared in the extracted clean text.

  2. Business population (HMRC official statistics, cross-checked): ~7.0m individuals in Self Assessment with landlord/sole-trader businesses in 2023–24; ~2.9m (42%) have qualifying income above £20,000 and must join MTD. Split: 864,000 (>£50k, Apr 2026), 1,077,000 (£30k–£50k, Apr 2027), 975,000 (£20k–£30k, Apr 2028).

  3. PDF table extraction (with pdfplumber): the HMRC "Income tax receipts: analysis by type" PDF (Table 2.8) has dense multi-year tables with merged cells. Default cell extraction returned empty rows; switching to layout-aware text strategy produced the full Self Assessment net receipts series 2007–08 to 2018–19.

  4. Charting (with plotly): two interactive charts below were generated as standalone HTML chunks and embedded into this report — one from the pdfplumber-extracted PDF numbers, one from the MTD population statistics.

Library validation notes

  • trafilatura 2.0.0: extracted 7,232 chars from the GOV.UK eligibility guidance and 7,968 chars from the "Use MTD" intro — nav/related-links stripped, headings and tables preserved.
  • pdfplumber 0.11.5: opened the 2-page HMRC Table 2.8 PDF and located the tables; extract_text() gave clean columnar rows. Lesson learned: default extract_tables() struggles with merged cells — the vertical_strategy: "text" option extracted the numbers reliably.
  • plotly 6.9.0: both charts render interactively (hover, zoom) and embed into the single-file HTML via to_html(full_html=False, include_plotlyjs="cdn").

Gaps and limitations

  • MTD population figures are based on tax year 2023–24 Self Assessment returns (latest available); actual joiners depend on returns for the relevant year.
  • The HMRC receipts PDF covers years to 2018–19 (a dated example chosen to test the pipeline, not current data).
  • This is a research-method test, not tax advice.
