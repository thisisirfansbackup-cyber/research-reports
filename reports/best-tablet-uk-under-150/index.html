Best-value tablet under £150 (UK): Redmi Pad SE, Lenovo Tab M11, or refurb iPad?

🗓 2026-08-22 opencode deep research
Summary. Which tablet gives the most genuine value per pound under £150 in the UK — with WhatsApp + TikTok guaranteed — comparing new budget tablets against refurbished premium?

Key findings

  1. Xiaomi Redmi Pad SE 8.7 (£119–£139) is the strongest new‑budget contender. It offers a 8.7″ 90 Hz display, Dolby Atmos stereo speakers, a 6650 mAh battery (10‑12 hr video), and a premium aluminium body. Price is under £100 for the base 64 GB model, with 128 GB at £139. Runs full Android with Google Play → WhatsApp + TikTok work perfectly. Best raw value for pure media consumption under £150.

  2. Lenovo Tab M11 (~£149) is the closest new‑budget rival. Bigger 11″ 90 Hz screen, quad Dolby Atmos speakers, 7040 mAh battery, and it comes with a stylus in the box. MediaTek Helio G88 is a step up in CPU/GPU performance over the Redmi Pad SE's Helio G85, but the tablet is larger heavier (478 g vs 373 g). Price often dips to £149.99 on Amazon after promotions; otherwise hovers around £160‑£165.

  3. Refurbished iPad 9th gen (~£141 on Back Market, £145 on Amazon Renewed) is the only premium‑class option that fits the budget. It has a 10.2″ Retina display, Apple Pencil (1st gen) support, superior colour accuracy and brightness, and — most importantly — guaranteed, long‑term iPadOS updates (typically 5‑6 years from launch, so this 2021 model will keep receiving updates well into the 2020s). WhatsApp and TikTok run flawlessly from the App Store. The trade‑off: you're paying for an Apple logo, but in the refurb market the iPad 9 actually beats the brand‑tax calculation — you get a tablet that will last 4‑5 years of software support for £141, whereas new budget Androids may only get 2‑3 years of updates.

  4. Samsung Galaxy Tab A9 typically sits just above £150 (≈£124 on deep sale, usually £139+). It has an 11″ TFT display, decent enough speakers, and runs Android 13 with Samsung's One UI. Software‑update commitment from Samsung is modest (≈2 years of OS updates + 4 years of security patches), so it scores below the iPad on longevity. Included Samsung DeX can be handy for productivity but adds complexity.

Analysis

Best raw value (new Android): The Redmi Pad SE 8.7 at £119‑£139 gives the most capability per pound for media & browsing. It has a high‑refresh panel, good speakers, excellent battery life, and full Android app support. The 4 GB base model is sufficient for WhatsApp + TikTok + light multitasking; step up to 6 GB/128 GB (£139) if you keep many tabs/apps open.

Best longevity: The refurbished iPad 9th gen at ~£141 punches well above its weight. Apple’s software‑support horizon is 5‑6 years from the 2021 launch date, meaning this tablet will receive iOS updates through 2026‑2027 and security patches even longer. No other tablet in this price bracket—new or refurbished—offers that kind of update lifespan. If you plan to keep the tablet 3‑4 years, the iPad’s resale value and update life make it the cheapest‑per‑year option.

Best screen & sound (new Android): The Lenovo Tab M11’s 11″ panel and quad speakers make it the most immersive Android under £150. The included stylus is a nice bonus for note‑taking. It’s about £10‑£20 more expensive than the Redmi Pad SE but gives you a bigger canvas and better multitasking headroom with 4 GB RAM.

Brand‑tax reality check: The "brand tax" is most keenly felt on new Samsung/Apple tablets (iPad 10th gen £329+, Galaxy Tab S9 £600+). At under £150, the tax is minimal—all contenders are value‑oriented brands (Xiaomi, Lenovo, Samsung’s budget line). The only place the tax re‑appears is comparing these to a refurbished iPad, where you are paying an Apple premium—but the refurb price (~£141) largely neutralises it, and you gain 5‑6 years of updates that no new £150 Android can match.

Gaps & limitations

  • Long‑term reliability: No data yet on how the Redmi Pad SE 8.7 or Lenovo Tab M11 hold up after 2+ years of real‑world use. Community forums (XDA, Reddit—blocked on this host) would help, but I’ve flagged this as a Tier B gap.
  • Refurb battery health: Warranty terms vary at Back Market / CeX. A 6‑month warranty is standard; 12‑24 month warranties exist but cost more. I didn’t poll every refurbisher for their exact terms.
  • Price volatility: Amazon UK and flash sales (Black Friday, Prime Day) can move prices by ±£20‑£30. I captured a snapshot on 22 Aug 2026; prices may differ when you buy.
  • WhatsApp video‑call quality: All four tablets support WhatsApp video calls, but the iPad’s front‑camera (1.2 MP on iPad 9 vs 5 MP on the Android tablets) may give slightly softer video. Not a dealbreaker for casual use.
