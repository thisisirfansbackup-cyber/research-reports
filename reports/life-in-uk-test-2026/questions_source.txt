Lesson 1

1. **Question:** What are two responsibilities that you have as a British citizen or a permanent resident of the UK?
**Answers:** (1) To look after the area in which you live and the environment. (2) To look after yourself and your family.
**Explanation:** Whether you reside permanently or hold British citizenship, your duties are to take care of your local surroundings and look after yourself and your family.

2. **Question:** Where is Big Ben located?
**Answer:** At the Houses of Parliament.

3. **Question:** When were men and women given the right to vote at the age of 21?
**Answer:** 1928.
**Explanation:** This was the year voting rights were equalized for both men and women at age 21.

4. **Question:** Where is the UK geographically located?
**Answer:** In the north-west of Europe.

5. **Question:** Who is the patron saint of Scotland?
**Answer:** St Andrew.

6. **Question:** Which two houses fought in the Wars of the Roses?
**Answers:** The House of York and The House of Lancaster.
**Explanation / Tip:** When the question asks for two, you must select both options, otherwise the answer is marked wrong.

7. **Question:** True or False: The Bill of Rights of 1689 confirmed the right to vote for all adult men.
**Answer:** False.
**Explanation:** The Bill of Rights did not grant universal voting rights; it confirmed the powers of Parliament and limited the powers of the King.

8. **Question:** What is the Cenotaph?
**Answer:** A war memorial.

9. **Question:** Who was the first female Prime Minister of the UK?
**Answer:** Margaret Thatcher.

10. **Question:** Who chairs the debates at the House of Commons?
**Answer:** The Speaker.
**Explanation:** The Speaker is the person who presides over debates and sessions in the Commons.

11. **Question:** Who was the captain of the English football team that won the World Cup in 1966?
**Answer:** Bobby Moore.

12. **Question:** When did the English defeat the Spanish Armada?
**Answer:** 1588.
**Explanation:** "Defeat" means to beat; the English forces defeated the Spanish Armada in 1588.

13. **Question:** True or False: Anyone can make a complaint about the police by writing to the chief constable of the police force involved.
**Answer:** True.
**Explanation / Tip:** Because it says "anyone", it is true. If it had stated that you "can only" complain this way, it would be false because other complaint channels exist.

14. **Question:** What is the minimum age required to serve on a jury?
**Answer:** 18 years old.

15. **Question:** How is a jury selected?
**Answer:** Randomly from the electoral register.
**Explanation:** Jurors are chosen at random from the registered voter list.

16. **Question:** What is a bank holiday?
**Answer:** A public holiday when banks and many other businesses are closed for the day.

17. **Question:** What will you be given to vote before a general election takes place?
**Answer:** A poll card.

18. **Question:** Which statement about solicitors is correct?
**Answer:** Solicitors usually charge based on how much time is spent on a case.
**Explanation:** They bill by the time spent rather than the importance or nature of the case.

19. **Question:** By law, which two types of media have to give balanced coverage to all political parties and equal time to rival viewpoints before an election?
**Answers:** Television and Radio.
**Explanation / Tip:** Make sure to select both media types, as the law requires equal and balanced coverage across both.

20. **Question:** Which territory is a Crown dependency but is not part of the UK?
**Answer:** The Channel Islands (or Isle of Man).

**Answer:** The jet engine.

22. **Question:** Who wrote Daffodils?
**Answer:** William Wordsworth.

**Answer:** Oliver Cromwell.

24. **Question:** What are the 40 days before Easter called?
**Answer:** Lent.

LESSON 17

1. **Question:** Which of the following is NOT a constitutional institution?
**Answer:** The armed forces.
**Explanation:** Constitutional institutions in the UK comprise Parliament, the Judiciary, and the Monarchy; the armed forces are not a constitutional branch of government.

2. **Question:** What food shortage caused the Great Famine in Ireland during the mid-19th century?
**Answer:** Potatoes (due to potato crop blight).

3. **Question:** Senior opposition MPs chosen to challenge government policy and propose alternative policies form a group known as:
**Answer:** The Shadow Cabinet.

4. **Question:** Which of the following are two responsibilities of MPs (Members of Parliament)?
**Answers:** (1) To represent everyone in their constituency. (2) To help create new laws.

5. **Question:** Who was the longest-serving Prime Minister of the 20th century?
**Answer:** Margaret Thatcher.

6. **Question:** What name is given to territories that are linked to the Crown but are not part of the United Kingdom?
**Answer:** Crown dependencies (such as the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands).

7. **Question:** To which area of study did David Hume contribute during the Scottish Enlightenment?
**Answer:** Philosophy.
**Explanation:** David Hume was a Scottish Enlightenment thinker celebrated for his contributions to philosophy.

8. **Question:** What proportion of the population died in England as a result of the Black Death?
**Answer:** One-third (1/3) of the population.

9. **Question:** What happened to King Charles I after being imprisoned during the English Civil War?
**Answer:** He was executed.

10. **Question:** What are two constitutional responsibilities of the Monarch?
**Answers:** (1) To appoint the government. (2) To advise, warn, and encourage the Prime Minister regarding government policies.

11. **Question:** What kind of disaster was the Black Death?
**Answer:** A plague (epidemic disease).

12. **Question:** Which country was Britain in frequent conflict with over global trade and colonies during the 18th century?
**Answer:** France.

13. **Question:** True or False: The Speaker of the House of Commons is an MP who represents a constituency and deals with constituents' issues.
**Answer:** True.
**Explanation:** Although the Speaker resigns from party politics to remain impartial in debates, they remain an MP with normal constituency representation duties.

14. **Question:** Where is Skara Brae, northern Europe's best-preserved prehistoric village, located?
**Answer:** In Scotland (Orkney).

15. **Question:** Which of the following plays was written by William Shakespeare?
**Answer:** Macbeth.

16. **Question:** Where was William the Conqueror from?
**Answer:** Normandy, France.

17. **Question:** What were the settlers of Dutch origin called who fought against the British in South Africa?
**Answer:** The Boers.
**Explanation:** The conflicts between the British Empire and these settlers in South Africa were known as the Boer Wars.

18. **Question:** True or False: In Northern Ireland, Youth Court cases are heard by up to three specially trained magistrates or a District Judge.
**Answer:** True.

19. **Question:** What achievement made Sir Roger Bannister famous?
**Answer:** He was the first person in the world to run a mile in under four minutes (in 1954).

20. **Question:** Who was invited to invade England and take the throne in 1688 during the Glorious Revolution?
**Answer:** William of Orange (William III).

21. **Question:** Who was defeated at the Battle of the Boyne in 1690?
**Answer:** King James II.
**Explanation:** James II was defeated in Ireland by William of Orange (William III).

22. **Question:** What did the Factories Act of 1847 introduce?
**Answer:** It limited the maximum working hours for women and children in textile mills to 10 hours a day (The Ten Hours Act).

23. **Question:** What name is given to the group of MPs from the official opposition who scrutinize government departments?
**Answer:** The Shadow Cabinet.

24. **Question:** In which of the following devolved areas can the Scottish Parliament legislate?
**Answer:** Education.
**Explanation:** Education is fully devolved to the Scottish Parliament, allowing it to make its own laws and policies in Scotland.
