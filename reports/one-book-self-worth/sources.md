# Sources — One Book to Address Foundational Weaknesses

Accessed 2026-08-15.

## Primary book candidates
1. [The Nice Guy Syndrome | No More Mr. Nice Guy official site](https://nomoremrniceguy.com/the-nice-guy-syndrome/) — author's own description of the syndrome and what the book teaches (boundaries, purpose, shame, anxiety). Accessed 2026-08-15.
2. [The Mr. Nice Guy Syndrome and Adverse Childhood Experiences — Psychology Today (2024)](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/hidden-wounds/202405/the-mr-nice-guy-syndrome-and-adverse-childhood-experiences) — clinician framing of the syndrome: toxic shame, approval-seeking, conflict avoidance, corrective strategies.
3. [6 Signs of Nice Guy Syndrome — Men's Health (2023)](https://www.menshealth.com/health/a43216627/6-definitive-signs-of-nice-guy-syndrome/) — licensed therapists (Tzall, LePera, Smith) on symptoms, covert contracts, and how to break the pattern.
4. [No More Mr. Nice Guy — Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/97642.No_More_Mr_Nice_Guy) — rating ~4.04/5 from ~25,000 ratings; also see [editions page](https://www.goodreads.com/work/editions/94130-no-more-mr-nice-guy).
5. [No More Mr. Nice Guy — Google Books / publisher record](https://www.google.com/books/edition/No_More_Mr_Nice_Guy/qWkHAAAACAAJ?hl=en) — bibliographic details; published Running Press 2003.
6. [Review, Context, and Reception — Shortform (2021)](https://www.shortform.com/blog/no-more-mr-nice-guy-review/) — balanced reception: 76% five-star Amazon, critiques (vagueness, gender essentialism), "Breaking Free Activities," partner involvement.
7. [No More Mr. Nice Guy review and people pleasing patterns — Dr. A. R. Graff (2026)](https://drarigraff.com/reclaim-your-life-how-to-break-free-from-people-pleasing/) — therapist review: NMMNG accurately names the patterns; pairs with therapy.
8. [A Psychology of Men? Critical Review of Robert Glover — Richard Carrier (2021)](https://www.richardcarrier.info/archives/18508) — major skeptic critique: no citations to research, anecdotal evidence, gender essentialism.
9. [No More Mr. Nice Guy — AudioFile review (2005)](https://www.audiofilemagazine.com/reviews/read/18003/no-more-mr-nice-guy-by-robert-a-glover-read-by-robert-okeefe/) — negative: "tired generalizations," oversimplification.
10. [No More Mr. Nice Guy: A Book Review — The Good Men Project (2016)](https://goodmenproject.com/featured-content/no-more-mr-nice-guy-a-book-review-jgc/) — positive review: meeting your own needs, "healthily selfish," group reading.
11. [No More Mr. Nice Guy — MensGroup.com summary (2020)](https://mensgroup.com/no-more-mr-nice-guy-book-summary/) — men's group framing: boundaries, backbone.
12. [Therapist endorsements](https://www.jesseking.com/specialties/no-more-mr-nice-guy-therapist/) (Jesse King LMFT), [drpariser.com For Men](https://drpariser.com/for-men) (Michael Pariser), [Alister Gates](https://alistergates.com/no-more-mr-nice-guy-2/), [doctornikolaidis.com](https://www.doctornikolaidis.com/no-more-mr-nice-guy), [changingforgood.com (Peter Hannah, LMHC)](https://www.changingforgood.com/counseling-for-nice-guys-and-people-pleasers/), [Phoenix Men's Counseling](https://www.phoenixmenscounseling.com/blog/2011/11/10/dealing-with-the-nice-guy-syndrome) — independent therapists who work with this population using NMMNG.

## Root-cause science (contingent self-worth)
18. [Contingencies of Self-Worth — Crocker & Wolfe, Psychological Review (2001)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1111/j.0963-7214.2005.00364.x) — self-worth staked on external domains = psychological vulnerability; costs to autonomy, relationships, self-regulation.
19. [Self-esteem and "if…then" contingencies of interpersonal acceptance — PubMed (1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8979382/) — low self-esteem associated with linking acceptance/rejection to success/failure.
20. [Contingencies of self-worth and responses to negative interpersonal feedback — Park et al. (2008)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/15298860701398808) — approval-based self-worth → bigger drops in state self-esteem/affect after negative feedback.
21. [Contingent self-esteem and the interpersonal circumplex — ScienceDirect (2006)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0191886905002898) — others'-approval contingency associated with nurturant+submissive interpersonal style.
22. [Contingencies of self-worth, suspicion, and authenticity in partner affirmation — Lemay & Clark, JESP (2008)](https://clarkrelationshiplab.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/You%27re%20just%20saying%20that_%20Contingencies%20of%20self-worth%2C%20suspicion%2C%20and%20authenticity%20in%20the%20interpersonal%20affirmation%20process.pdf) — contingent self-worth undermines trust in partner feedback; partner detects it.
23. [The relationship of social approval contingency to trait self-esteem — ScienceDirect (2005)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0092656605000024) — approval contingency linked to lower self-esteem.
