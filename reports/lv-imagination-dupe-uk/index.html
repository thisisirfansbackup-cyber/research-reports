Key findings

  1. Rasasi Hawas Kobra (100ml) at £26.20 from Notino UK — £28.69 delivered — is the only option that satisfies both hard constraints. It shares five of Imagination's distinctive notes, and independent Fragrantica users report 8h, 8–9h, "8+ hours even on my pretty dry skin", and 12h on skin [Tier B].   2. Arabiyat Prestige Marwa is the most accurate clone in existence — and it fails the 8-hour requirement. Its note list is a near-exact copy of Imagination's, down to the same origin adjectives (Calabrian bergamot, Nigerian ginger, Ceylon cinnamon, Tunisian neroli, Chinese black tea, Ambroxan, guaiac wood, olibanum) [Tier A].   3. Al Haramain Amber Oud Aqua Dubai is the community's top pick and the strongest performer, but misses the budget by 99p and is structurally the least similar. 75ml is £33.50 at Notino, £35.99 delivered at the cheapest (collection) option [Tier A, verified live]. Skin reports: 8+h, 9–10h, 12+h, 24h [Tier B]. Yet it contains no tea, no ginger and no cinnamon — it is a fruity-aquatic (melon, pineapple, blackcurrant) that reads similar in the air rather than a structural clone [Tier A].
  4. Several widely circulated longevity numbers were measured on clothing, not skin. The blind-test channel's "6–6.5 hours" for both Kobra and Marwa is explicitly "when applied on the clothes" [Tier B, transcript]. On-clothes figures routinely run 2–4× the on-skin figure, so quoting them against a skin requirement overstates performance badly.
  5. Four brands named in the original brief are not Imagination clones at all: Afnan Supremacy in Heaven (a Creed Silver Mountain Water dupe), Zara Sunrise on the Red Sand Dunes (now reads as L'Immensité, and is unbuyable on Zara UK), Dossier (no Imagination dupe on its UK site), and Lattafa Ansaam/Aura (Oriana and BR540 clones respectively).

Background: what LV Imagination is

Citrus Aromatic EDP by Jacques Cavallier-Belletrud, launched 3 June 2021 (not 2019). Fragrantica rating 4.54/5 from 13,288 votes [Tier A].

Layer Notes
Top Citron, Calabrian bergamot, Sicilian orange
Heart Tunisian neroli, Nigerian ginger, Ceylon cinnamon
Base Chinese black tea, Ambroxan, guaiac wood, olibanum

The distinctive signature is the ginger + cinnamon + black tea heart over Ambroxan — that combination is what a clone has to reproduce. The original's own longevity is inconsistent: skin reports on Fragrantica range from 1–2h on some batches to 8–12h on others.

Recommendation

Buy Rasasi Hawas Kobra 100ml, £26.20 at Notino UK (£28.69 delivered).

It is the only candidate that satisfies both hard constraints simultaneously. On your stated priorities:

  1. Scent accuracy — it keeps Imagination's signature ginger–cinnamon–neroli–tea structure, and in the only blind test conducted against a real bottle it was judged closest of nine clones: "Rasasi Hawas Cobra, by far I felt is the closest to Imagination… when you smell them up close, Hawas Cobra may seem a bit closer to the OG." Marwa placed second there, described as "good in the dry down but not so good in the opening" [Tier B, transcript verified]. Be clear-eyed though: it is a twist, not a 1:1 — heavier, soapier and more amber-forward than the airy original.
  2. Formulation quality — Rasasi is an established Dubai house; Notino rating 4.6/5 from 33 verified buyers. The consistent criticism is a harsh opening of roughly 10–15 minutes (one buyer: "CHAOS… the opening was insanely intense"), which multiple users say settles substantially after 2–4 weeks of maceration. No reports of thin or watery construction.
  3. Longevity 8h+ — met, with the most direct evidence available: a user who "hiked for 8 hours continuously while wearing this and it's still on my skin at the 8th hour even after excessive" sweating, plus independent 8–9h and 12h skin reports and a 4.6/5 longevity score from buyers.

Honest caveats on this pick. Its promoters are affiliate-heavy, and per your own check it has no Reddit footprint — so its support comes from Fragrantica users, verified Notino buyers and one blind test, not from the subreddit. There is real dissent (5–6h, and 2h in extreme heat). It is also the least accurate of the two structural clones.

If you can stretch by £1: Al Haramain Amber Oud Aqua Dubai 75ml, £35.99 delivered

This is the community consensus pick and the strongest performer in the entire field — 8+ to 24 hours on skin, with a 4.5/5 longevity score from 40 buyers. If accuracy matters more than the 8-hour rule: Arabiyat Prestige Marwa, £29.99

The closest thing to Imagination money can buy at this price — a near note-for-note reconstruction. But the skin evidence is genuinely poor (2–6h typical), so it would break your hard requirement. Only choose it if you would rather smell right for six hours than close for nine.

Practical advice

  • Macerate before judging. Every one of these Arabic-house fragrances gets materially better 2–4 weeks after opening. Do not write off a bottle on day one.
  • Spray fewer than you think. Kobra and Aqua Dubai are both described as room-filling; 2–4 sprays is the repeated advice.
  • Batch variance is unavoidable. The £255 original itself swings from 1–2h to 8–12h between batches. No clone can guarantee 8 hours on a specific bottle and a specific skin.
  • Notino's 90-day returns apply if the bottle disappoints, which de-risks both Notino picks.

Gaps and limitations

  • Reddit remains inaccessible from this system — every public route returns a block page, including via the search index. The Reddit consensus reported here (Aqua Dubai as top pick; Hawas Kobra with zero mentions) was supplied by the user, not independently verified, and is labelled as such. It is directionally consistent with the Fragrantica forum record, where Aqua Dubai is also the favourite.
  • Fragrantica's longevity/sillage bar charts are JavaScript-rendered and genuinely not present in the page source. All figures here are individual user claims mined from review text, with counts stated — not site aggregates.
  • The blind test is a single pair of noses, tested on strips at drydown, in a hot climate, with performance measured on clothing. It is the best direct comparison available but it is not a controlled study.
  • "8 hours" is nobody's stopwatch measurement. All longevity data in this field is self-reported and subject to nose fatigue. The substrate separation above removes the largest systematic error, not all error.
  • Prices are a snapshot. Both Notino prices are explicitly limited-time sales; the Karus delivery figure is estimated rather than taken from a basket.
  • Vendor and affiliate sources (Scento, ScentClones, SARA SAKINA, and the dupe houses' own blogs) were used for discovery and are labelled Tier C. None is the sole support for any claim above.
