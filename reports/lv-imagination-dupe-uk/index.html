Background: what LV Imagination actually is

Louis Vuitton Imagination is a Citrus Aromatic Eau de Parfum for men, created by in-house master perfumer Jacques Cavallier-Belletrud and launched 3 June 2021 (some sources loosely date it 2019; the verifiable launch is 2021 — the seventh men's fragrance in LV's Les Parfums collection) [4][5][8][9].

Notes pyramid (confirmed across LV press copy and Fragrantica):

Layer Notes
Top Calabrian bergamot, Sicilian orange, citron
Heart Tunisian neroli, Nigerian ginger (CO₂-extracted), Ceylon cinnamon
Base Chinese black tea (CO₂-extracted), Ambroxan, guaiac wood, olibanum

Key accords (Fragrantica): citrus (100), fresh spicy (36), green (28), fresh (28), amber (12), aromatic (12) [4]. The "Ceylon tea" descriptor sometimes used in the brief is a conflation — the tea is Chinese black tea; Ceylon refers to the cinnamon. "Ambrette" and "iris" notes are not documented in any reliable source.

Original performance (context for what a dupe must beat): Fragrantica community votes and independent reviews place the original at roughly 7–12h longevity (typically 8–10h) with moderate sillage [4][11][12]. Notably, even the original shows severe batch variance — from ~1–2h on some batches to 8–12h on others [4]. This matters: no fragrance, original or clone, can guarantee 8h on every batch and every skin. The community consensus is that an 8h performer among clones exists, but batch-testing is advised.

The alternative picks and when to choose them

Choose Arabiyat Prestige Marwa if absolute scent accuracy trumps everything and you accept longevity risk. It is the 2026 consensus #1 for similarity (85–95%; Fragrantica users describe the drydown as 90–95% similar) [2][13][16][18]. But its longevity evidence is genuinely split — ScentClones/Scento claim 8–12h, while multiple Fragrantica users report 1–3h on skin [14][18]. If you buy it, buy from Gadcet (£29.99, free delivery, 30-day returns [2]) and test a fresh batch; maceration typically helps. Its atomiser is also weaker (pressurised-but-unpopular sprayer) [15].

Choose Khadlaj Karus Blu Spice if you want the cheapest strong performer and don't mind a looser scent. At ~£27 delivered it is the best value per ml, and when it performs it outperforms everything (9–13h claims) [3][13]. But community reviews are split between "best clone I've put my nose on" and "not the best or closest as a clone… a banger as a standalone" — the extra cinnamon/spice makes it the least faithful of the three [20]. Some report under 2h skin-scent, so batch variance is real [20].

Analysis and implications

  • The 8-hour requirement reshapes the whole shortlist. Most "obvious" UK-buyable dupes (Zara, Dossier, the UK dupe houses) are either not Imagination clones at all or don't reach 8 hours. The three candidates that clear £35 and clear 8 hours are all Middle-Eastern houses — this is where the 2024–2026 clone market has consolidated.
  • Batch variance is the biggest single risk. Even the original LV ranges from 1–2h to 8–12h by batch. All three shortlisted clones have some batch/longevity variance in the community record; maceration (letting the bottle rest 2–4 weeks after opening) is repeatedly recommended and reliably improves both accuracy and longevity for these Arabic EDPs [1][18][21].
  • Scent accuracy vs longevity is a real trade-off at this price point. The single most accurate clone (Marwa) is also the one with the most contested longevity; the most consistent 8h+ performer (Karus Blu Spice) is the loosest scent. Hawas Kobra is the best intersection of all three priorities — near-top accuracy with verified 8–10h longevity.
