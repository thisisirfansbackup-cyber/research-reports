Is Yoga Actually Beneficial? A Critical Review of the Academic Evidence

2026-08-16 · open report

Across roughly 300 randomized trials and dozens of meta-analyses, yoga shows small-to-moderate benefits for depression, anxiety, stress, chronic low back pain, blood pressure, and quality of life — but almost always against "doing nothing," not against other exercise. Against active comparators (aerobic exercise, stretching, physiotherapy, attention controls) yoga is equivalent at best and non-superior at worst. Evidence quality is predominantly GRADE "low" to "very low"; the specific claim that yoga outperforms ordinary exercise is not supported by high-certainty evidence. It is a safe, acceptable form of structured physical activity plus relaxation — not a magic bullet.
